## Story

    A young king or queen inherits the realm their father left behind — modest, untested, and already in the sights of a neighbouring warlord.
    You must build your kingdom from the ground up: fill the treasury, grow your population, earn the loyalty of your people and nobles, forge alliances, and open trade routes across the land.
    You have **THREE YEARS**.
    To the east, the warlords of **Vorreth** grow bold. Their spies watch your every move. Their armies are already forming.
    When the three years are up, they march. Will you be prepared — or will you perish?

---

## Random Events

Every two seasons, a random event forces a tough choice. There are no free options — every decision costs something.

- **Noble's Grand Feast** — Fund it and nobles love you, but the people resent the excess.
- **Bandit Raid** — Send knights, pay bounties, or look the other way. Each has consequences.
- **Monster Attack** — A creature terrorises outlying villages. Ignore it and lose reputation fast.
- **Plague Scare** — Fund healers, quarantine, or do nothing. Population is on the line.
- **Harvest Festival** — Your people want to celebrate. Will you pay for it?
- **Drought** — Dry summer threatens food supply. Import grain or ration — and suffer for it.
